# APEX Refinement Audit — 390 × 844

## What the current page communicates

The current first fold identifies APEX and FC Mobile clearly, but its editorial headline appears before the account artifact. The image therefore reads as a supporting showcase rather than the product’s proof of intent. The stack is legible and technically sound, yet its three-stage rhythm—statement, concept card, feature list—still resembles a premium landing template more than a specialist mobile product.

## Concrete refinement decisions

| Audit question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| What should the eye see first? | A labelled **platform specimen** should enter within the first glance, with its account metadata living on the visual itself. |
| How should APEX identify itself? | Shift from a small logo-and-headline system to a recognisable account grammar: `APEX / A-001`, `OVR`, resource marks, status and coordinate rails. |
| How should the product story scroll? | Treat the Home page as a single inspection sequence: artefact → account grammar → platform protocol → community wire. |
| What becomes less generic? | Remove explanatory feature-card language; turn capability messaging into concise interface protocol and documentation vocabulary. |
| What creates mobile tension? | Let the product frame breach the layout boundary, overlap the hero copy, react to press, and transition into the following system readout. |

## Guardrails

All numeric account fields will remain dashed or explicitly labelled as platform concept values. No live inventory, price, sale evidence, community URL, or customer claim will be fabricated.
